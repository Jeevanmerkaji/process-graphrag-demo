# Quality Gate Review (wiki page)

*Synthetic example document for demonstration purposes.*

The quality gate review is held at each major milestone of the product development process, for example concept release, design freeze and production release.

The **project manager** prepares the Gate Review Package, which consolidates the status of all deliverables: Design FMEA status, open change requests, the Simulation Report and the Test Report. Collecting these deliverables takes two to three days per gate because the information sits in the PLM system, the FMEA software, the test data management system and in e-mails.

In the review meeting the **gate review board** (quality, engineering, project management) evaluates the deliverables against the Gate Criteria Checklist and decides: pass, conditional pass with actions, or fail. The decision is documented in the Gate Protocol.

**Known problems:** the gate criteria are interpreted differently between projects. Open actions from earlier gates are not systematically followed up by the project manager.
