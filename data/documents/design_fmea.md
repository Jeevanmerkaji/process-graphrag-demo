# Work Instruction WI-DEV-014: Design FMEA

*Synthetic example document for demonstration purposes.*

**Purpose:** systematic analysis of potential failure modes of a new or changed design.
**Scope:** all new components and all changes approved by the Change Control Board.

## Steps

1. **Define scope and structure.** The FMEA moderator sets up the system structure from the Bill of Materials in the FMEA software. Output: Structure Analysis.
2. **Function and failure analysis.** In a workshop, design engineers and the FMEA moderator derive functions, failure modes, effects and causes. Engineers should reuse lessons learned from previous FMEAs and field failures, but these are spread over hundreds of FMEA files and are hard to find.
3. **Risk assessment.** The team rates severity, occurrence and detection and computes the action priority. The Simulation Report from virtual validation and the Test Report from prototype testing are used as evidence for the occurrence and detection ratings.
4. **Define and track actions.** For a high action priority, the design engineer defines actions such as a design change or an additional test. Actions are tracked in the Design FMEA until closed. Tracking is manual and open actions are often forgotten.
5. **Release.** The quality engineer reviews and releases the Design FMEA. Its status is an input to the quality gate review.
